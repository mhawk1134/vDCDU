## In plain words

This summary isn't part of the licences; the texts above and in `firmware/LICENSE.md` are what count.

- **Flying at home, streaming, sharing a video of your cockpit:** all fine.
- **Building your own display:** fine. Change the firmware as much as you like, and share your changes (for example a port to another board) under the same licence.
- **Sharing:** share the link to this page. You may pass on the unmodified setup program for free.
- **Selling:** selling the program, the firmware, or displays or kits that run it needs written permission. Ask in [Discussions](../../discussions).
